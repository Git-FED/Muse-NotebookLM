Small tool. Big note energy.

Notes out.
Plain text in.

muse-nlm turns readable NotebookLM page content into plain text you can pipe anywhere. No browser automation. No extra packages. Just one tiny tool doing its one tiny job.

your very normal terminal
# retrieve readable page text
$ nlm open https://notebooklm.google.com/notebook/<id>

# and send it wherever you like
$ nlm open <url> | grep "important bit"
